
# GulfBridge Talent

**Verified Talent. Reliable Mobilization.**

GulfBridge Talent is an independent international
workforce recruitment and mobilization company
headquartered in Lucknow, India.

Founded on 18 November 2023, GulfBridge Talent
connects employers across the GCC with skilled
talent sourced from India.

## Our Expertise

- International workforce recruitment
- Skilled trade sourcing and verification
- Construction and engineering manpower
- GCC workforce mobilization
- Workforce planning and procurement
- Candidate screening and documentation

## GCC Markets

UAE | Saudi Arabia | Qatar | Kuwait | Oman | Bahrain
